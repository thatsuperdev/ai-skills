# Example fills

## Wireless earbuds case in clear sapphire crystal
- **Title:** "Case, Assembled"
- **Object:** a premium earbuds charging case, about 50×60×25 mm.
- **Material:** clear sapphire crystal. Accents: polished steel hinge pin and a made-up badge plate.
- **Inner parts:** charging PCB, battery pouch, pogo pins, hinge spring, magnets, two earbuds.
- **Alive moment:** a light wave runs from the USB-C port through the PCB traces into both earbuds, then the status LED pulses on the drop.
- **Sound cues:** clicks, magnetic snaps, a spring tick, a small screw.
- **Music mood:** clean, modern, rhythmic.
- **Build order:**
  1. bottom shell
  2. battery
  3. PCB (macro hero shot)
  4. pogo pins
  5. magnets
  6. hinge and spring
  7. lid
  8. earbuds drop in
- **In use:** the lid snaps open and an earbud lifts out.
- **Length:** 28 s at 60 fps, 1080×1350.
